Terms (pilot)

CatalogIQ is a brand of Apex Grid Engineering PLLC. Contact: info@catalogiqhq.com. These plain-language pilot terms are summarised here; the subscription agreement a pilot merchant signs is the binding document.

What CatalogIQ does

CatalogIQ drafts product listings from data the merchant supplies, normalises that data to store formats, and delivers or publishes listings only after the merchant approves them.

Fact lock and merchant responsibility

CatalogIQ does not invent product attributes. Factual content in a listing comes from the merchant's supplied data; anything missing is flagged, not filled in. The merchant reviews and approves every listing and remains responsible for the accuracy and legality of all product claims in their store — including regulated claims (health, safety, food, children's products, electrical or chemical), which require the merchant's explicit attestation before publication.

No outcome guarantees

CatalogIQ sells listing production. It does not guarantee search ranking, traffic, conversion, or sales.

Pilot pricing

Starter $99/mo (up to 500 active SKUs), Growth $199/mo (up to 2,500), Scale $399/mo (up to 10,000). ShopTranslate add-on: +$79/mo per language per store. Catalog Studio engagements are scoped in writing before work begins. Pilot pricing is test pricing and may be revised for new terms with notice; a merchant's current paid term is honoured as sold.

Payouts to partners (if ever applicable)

CatalogIQ's core service pays nobody out. If a future partner or seller-side arrangement ever involves CatalogIQ paying a recipient, the recipient chooses from the options offered at that time — bank payout via Stripe Connect, PayPal Payouts, or Plaid-linked direct ACH where implemented — with any limits (including PayPal's house processing cap) disclosed before the recipient earns.

Cancellation

A merchant may cancel at the end of a paid period. CatalogIQ stops imports and publishing, the merchant exports their full catalog and change log, and house copies are deleted as described in the Privacy page.
