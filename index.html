Straight answers

Questions merchants ask first.

What does CatalogIQ actually do?

It turns supplier spreadsheets into store-ready product listings: normalised titles, descriptions, SEO titles and meta, tags, and category mapping for your store — delivered as a platform-ready file or published to your connected Shopify store after you approve it.

Will it invent product details?

No. Every factual attribute — material, dimensions, compatibility, certifications — is locked to your supplied data. Missing fields are flagged in a completeness report for you to fill, never guessed. Drafting changes the wording, never the facts.

Which platforms are supported?

Any platform that imports a product CSV, plus direct store-connect publishing for Shopify at pilot. Additional platform field maps are added on pilot demand and are only listed here once they are real and verified.

What is ShopTranslate?

CatalogIQ's multilingual line: your approved listings translated and localised into another language for +$79/mo per language, per store. Product facts read identically in every language, regulated claims need your approval per language, and nothing publishes unapproved.

How does the pilot start?

Request access below, then send a sample of your supplier sheet. We draft 25 of your own SKUs as a free sample pack so you can judge real output on your real products before subscribing at $99–$399/mo.

Who owns my catalog data?

You do. Your catalog is used only to produce your listings — never shared with another merchant, never used in marketing without your written consent, and exportable in full if you cancel.
